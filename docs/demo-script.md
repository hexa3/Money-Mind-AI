# 5-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:30 Opening
"Students spend years in school but graduate unprepared for real money decisions. MoneyMind AI is Duolingo for real-life financial intelligence."

## 0:30-1:20 Landing and Dashboard
Show the landing page, then dashboard. Point out XP, streaks, quests, achievements, and the four-score model: money, happiness, knowledge, risk.

## 1:20-2:30 AI Life Simulator
Open `/simulator`. Customize the learner profile. Choose the "guaranteed crypto offer" or emergency repair scenario. Show how the consequence engine updates scores, explains the risk, awards XP, and unlocks achievements.

## 2:30-3:15 Scam Detector
Open `/scam-detector`. Scan the prefilled crypto message. Emphasize the scam score, suspicious phrases, and safety move.

## 3:15-4:00 Future Self Visualizer
Open `/future`. Change monthly savings and show the chart moving. Explain that students can see how small habits compound into milestones.

## 4:00-4:40 Teacher Dashboard
Open `/teacher`. Show classroom mastery, at-risk learners, leaderboard, and assigned challenges.

## 4:40-5:00 Close
"MoneyMind teaches finance the way students actually learn: by making choices, seeing consequences, and improving before real money is on the line."
