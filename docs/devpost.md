# MoneyMind AI Devpost Draft

## Inspiration
Students graduate knowing formulas but not how to handle debt, scams, credit, taxes, investing, or the emotional pressure of money decisions. We wanted finance education to feel like practice, not a lecture.

## What it does
MoneyMind AI is a gamified financial-literacy platform. Students enter a profile, then play an AI life simulator where every decision changes money, happiness, knowledge, and risk. The app also includes quests, an AI tutor, a scam detector, a future-self visualizer, and a teacher dashboard.

## How we built it
Next.js 15 App Router, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Framer Motion, Zustand, Recharts, Auth.js, Supabase/PostgreSQL schema, and OpenAI-ready server actions. The prototype includes deterministic fallback AI logic so the demo works without API keys.

## What makes it different
Most finance apps track money after the fact. MoneyMind lets students rehearse financial consequences before real life charges interest.

## Challenges
The hardest product challenge was making financial education understandable in under five minutes without making it feel childish. We used simulator choices, visual scoring, and classroom analytics to make the learning loop obvious.

## What is next
More scenario packs, real classroom rostering, deeper RLS policies, parent-facing summaries, tax simulations, and curriculum exports for teachers.
